# ECONOMICS.md

## Estimated Lead Value

The most valuable users for Spendora are startups or engineering teams already spending heavily on AI tooling across subscriptions and APIs.

If a company spends roughly $2,000–$5,000/month on AI tooling, a successful infrastructure credits or optimization deal could realistically generate several thousand dollars annually for Credex.

For rough estimation:
- average converted customer value: $5,000/year
- estimated gross margin: 20–40%

Even a relatively small number of successful conversions could make the tool financially useful.

---

## Customer Acquisition Cost

The initial acquisition strategy relies mostly on organic distribution channels.

Estimated early CAC:
- Twitter/X posts: near $0
- Product Hunt launch: near $0
- Reddit and founder communities: near $0
- direct founder outreach: mainly time cost

The MVP is designed to validate demand before spending heavily on paid acquisition.

---

## Conversion Funnel Assumptions

A reasonable early funnel could look like:

- 1000 landing page visitors
- 250 completed audits (25%)
- 40 email captures (16%)
- 10 consultation requests (25%)
- 2 paying customers (20%)

If each converted customer generates roughly $5,000/year in value:

2 customers × $5,000
= approximately $10,000 annual value from every 1000 visitors.

The economics work if customer value remains significantly higher than acquisition cost.

---

## Path Toward $1M ARR

To reach $1M ARR:

$1,000,000 / $5,000
≈ 200 active customers

That would require:
- consistent organic traffic
- strong audit completion rates
- reliable consultation bookings
- repeatable conversion into infrastructure or credits customers

If the platform generated a few hundred qualified leads per month and maintained steady conversion rates, the model could become financially meaningful over time.

---

## Why The Model Could Work

The core audit engine is deterministic and relatively inexpensive to run.

Most operational costs come from:
- AI-generated summaries
- hosting and database usage
- transactional email delivery

Because the product provides immediate value before lead capture, it has a better chance of converting users compared to traditional outbound sales funnels.
