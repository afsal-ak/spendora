## Interview 1

**Name:** P.P.  
**Role:** SQL Developer  
**Company Stage:** Mid-size healthcare software company

### Key Quotes
- “In our company we cannot directly use AI tools with confidential database information.”
- “Most SQL queries are still written manually. I sometimes use ChatGPT for reference, but I never paste full database code.”
- “If the report looked clean and trustworthy, I would probably share it internally with teammates.”

### Most Surprising Insight
The interviewee cared more about privacy and trust than savings recommendations.

### What It Changed
I started thinking more about how audit reports should avoid exposing sensitive company information in public share links.

## Interview 2

**Name:** P.  
**Role:** Startup Founder  
**Company Stage:** Early-stage startup

### Key Quotes
- “Right now we are building both a web and Flutter app for a travel platform, and we use OpenAI APIs for recommendations.”
- “Different people on the team use different AI tools, so subscriptions start adding up quickly.”
- “I would trust the audit more if the recommendations clearly explained why a cheaper option still fits the workflow.”

### Most Surprising Insight
The founder already knew AI costs were increasing but had never reviewed whether all subscriptions were still necessary.

### What It Changed
I improved the audit reasoning section so recommendations felt more transparent and financially realistic.

## Interview 3

**Name:** T.P.  
**Role:** Next.js Developer  
**Company Stage:** Startup

### Key Quotes
- “My company uses OpenAI APIs for chatbot suggestions and automatic itinerary generation. Personally, I mostly use ChatGPT Go.”
- “Most of the time I choose tools based on workflow comfort. For design I use Lovable, and for research I use Perplexity.”
- “I would probably use an audit tool once every few months, not daily.”

### Most Surprising Insight
The interviewee cared more about workflow convenience and speed than saving small amounts of money monthly.

### What It Changed
I focused more on workflow-fit recommendations instead of only showing savings numbers.
