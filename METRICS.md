# METRICS.md

## North Star Metric
Completed AI spend audits per week.

## Activation Metric
Percentage of visitors who successfully generate an audit.

## Conversion Metric
Percentage of users who submit their email after viewing audit results.

## Engagement Metrics
- Average audit completion time
- Most selected AI tools
- Shareable audit link usage

## Success Criteria
The MVP is successful if users:
- complete audits successfully
- trust the recommendations
- share or save audit results
- convert into leads
